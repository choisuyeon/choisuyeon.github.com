Short bio [third-person]
I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at Seoul National University, where I lead the SNU Reality Lab. My research focuses on developing novel algorithms and physical systems at the intersection of graphics, optics, artificial intelligence, and vision science. During my PhD, I developed holographic display systems incorporating machine learning towards next-generation VR/AR displays, and my thesis received the ACM SIGGRAPH Outstanding Dissertation Award Honorable Mention in 2025. My research has been supported by a Meta Research PhD Fellowship, a SPIE Optics and Photonics Education Scholarship, a Kwanjeong Scholarship, and a Korean Government Scholarship. I did my PhD at Stanford University with Prof. Gordon Wetzstein and undergrad at Seoul National University.
